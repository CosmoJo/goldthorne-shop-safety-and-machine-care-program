Emergency poster and machine cards

Page 1 is the emergency poster for the bulletin board outside the bathrooms. Then one page per machine card, to laminate and put at the machine, so the steps are there even without a phone. Cards are added as the machine stewards agree them. Each page has a code that opens the same information, kept up to date, on a phone.

Emergency

Call 911
Tell the 911 operator our address:
14 Goldthorne Avenue
Etobicoke, Toronto, Ontario
M8Z 5S8
Cross streets: Evans Avenue and Horner Avenue
Paramedics come in by the front door.
  1. Someone badly hurt, bleeding heavily, unconscious, burned or given an electric shock, or a fire: call 911 and give the address above. Send someone to the front door to meet the paramedics and bring them in.
  2. If a machine is involved, switch it off and keep everyone back. If someone has had an electric shock, don't touch them until the power is off.
  3. Heavy bleeding: press hard on the wound with anything clean. If blood soaks through, add more on top without lifting the first layer. Keep pressing until the paramedics take over.
  4. A finger or other part cut off: wrap it in a clean cloth, damp if you can, seal it in a plastic bag, and put that bag in a second bag or a container of ice and water. Never put it straight on ice. Write the time of the injury on the bag and give it to the paramedics.
  5. Keep the injured person lying down, still and warm, and give them nothing to eat or drink.
  6. Don't let an injured person drive themselves.
  7. Then tell a shop manager, and report it in the AI Assistant.

A fire you can't put out straight away: get everyone out, and call 911 from outside.

First aid kit: on the wall just outside the spray booth.

Drill press

Teknatool Nova Voyager DVR (58000)

Draft for the drill's machine stewards to agree at the drill.

Leave it like this

  1. Stop the drill and let the chuck come to a stop on its own. Don't grab it.
  2. Take the bit out, close the chuck's jaws, and put the chuck key back where it belongs.
  3. Let the handle return all the way up. Undo the knob that locks the handle down, and run the depth stop nut back up to the top.
  4. Put the control panel back to normal:
    • direction set to forward;
    • no drilling depth set;
    • Self-Start off;
    • normal speed: press F2.
    Self-Start makes the motor start on its own when someone pulls the handle down. If it's left on, the next person gets a surprise.
  5. Don't change anything in the menu. Changes there stay for everyone until someone resets the drill to its factory settings.
  6. Leave the table flat (not tilted), locked tight, at the usual height.
  7. Put the drill vise, fence and clamps back.
  8. Vacuum the chips off the drill, the table and the base. Don't use the air hose: dust that gets into the motor's sensors stops the drill.

Before you start

  1. Check the control panel: the speed you want, forward direction, and no drilling depth or Self-Start you didn't set yourself.
  2. Make sure the chuck key isn't in the chuck.
  3. Make sure the table is locked. With the power off, turn the bit by hand once to check it clears the work and the table.
  4. Lower the clear guard over the chuck. If the drill has the guard switch, it won't start with the guard up.
  5. Clamp the work or hold it in the drill vise, never in your hand.

Unplug the drill before doing any maintenance.

How to lock it out

  1. Press OFF and let the chuck stop.
  2. Pull the plug out of the wall.
  3. Keep the plug where you can see and reach it the whole time you work, or put a plug lock on it with your own lock and tag.
  4. Check it's dead: press ON. Nothing should happen.
  5. Unplugging covers waxing, greasing and changing the chuck. Never open the switch box or the rear covers: the maker says parts inside can carry dangerous voltage even when the drill is unplugged. That's work for a licensed electrician.
  6. To bring it back: tools and rags off the drill, guard down, then plug it in and start it with nobody near the chuck.

Parts and supplies

  • Teknatool part numbers:
    • chuck: 8338051;
    • chuck key: 8338052;
    • tapered shaft that holds the chuck in the drill: 8338004;
    • wedge for knocking that shaft out: 8338049.
  • To tilt the table: the wrench that came with the drill (part 8338048) and a 5 millimetre Allen key.
  • Once a month: paste wax on the bare metal surfaces.
  • Twice a year:
    • grease on the gear that raises the table and on the grooves of the spindle shaft;
    • one drop of light oil on the handle's gear.
  • To order parts: Teknatool, or King Canada (the Canadian importer). Check the model number on the drill's plate first: 58000 or 58006.
  • The maker says the drill must be plugged into a surge protector rated at least 15 amps.

Unsafe? Stop it, switch it off, tag it and tell a shop manager. Then scan the code to report it.
