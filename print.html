Emergency poster and machine cards

Page 1 is the emergency poster for the bulletin board outside the bathrooms. Then one page per machine card, to laminate and put at the machine, so the steps are there even without a phone. Every card is a draft for the machine's stewards to agree at the machine, and a steward replaces the printed copy when the card changes. Each page has a code that opens the same information, kept up to date, on a phone.

Emergency

Call 911
Tell the 911 operator our address:
14 Goldthorne Avenue
Etobicoke, Toronto, Ontario
M8Z 5S8
Cross streets: Evans Avenue and Horner Avenue
Paramedics come in by the front door.
  1. Someone badly hurt, bleeding heavily, unconscious, burned or given an electric shock, or a fire: call 911 and give the address above. Send someone to the front door to meet the paramedics and bring them in.
  2. If a machine is involved, switch it off and keep everyone back. If someone has had an electric shock, don't touch them until the power is off.
  3. Heavy bleeding: press hard on the wound with anything clean. If blood soaks through, add more on top without lifting the first layer. Keep pressing until the paramedics take over.
  4. A finger or other part cut off: wrap it in a clean cloth, damp if you can, seal it in a plastic bag, and put that bag in a second bag or a container of ice and water. Never put it straight on ice. Write the time of the injury on the bag and give it to the paramedics.
  5. Keep the injured person lying down, still and warm, and give them nothing to eat or drink.
  6. Don't let an injured person drive themselves.
  7. Then let the managers and first aiders know: send the emergency alert from the map's Emergency page (signed in to the AI Assistant), or tell a Wood Studio manager. Later, report what happened in the AI Assistant.

A fire you can't put out straight away: get everyone out, and call 911 from outside.

First aid kit: on the wall just outside the spray booth.

Sliding table saw

Martin T70 · Machine number 14

Draft for the sliding saw's machine stewards to agree at the saw. Martin's manual isn't on file yet, so this card comes from the program's record and general table saw guidance.

Leave it like this

  1. Stop the saw and let the blade come to a stop on its own.
  2. Lower the blade below the table.
  3. Bring the sliding table back to its resting position.
  4. Leave the riving knife fitted and the guard over the blade in place.
  5. Put the crosscut fence, stops and push sticks back where they belong.
  6. Brush offcuts and dust off the table. Never clear them by hand near the blade, and never with the air hose.
  7. Close blast gate G1 (the lever gate between the saw and the edge bander).

Before you start

  1. Open blast gate G1 and check the dust collector is running.
  2. Check the riving knife, the guard over the blade and the anti-kickback device are fitted and working.
  3. Fit a blade suited to the job, sharp and set. Raise it no more than about 3 millimetres (1/8 inch) above the wood.
  4. Lock the rip fence before cutting. For crosscuts on the sliding table, pull the rip fence back so the offcut can't jam between it and the blade.
  5. Set up support for long or wide panels before you start: extension tables or roller stands.
  6. Watch the power cable that runs across the floor from the yellow post: it's a trip hazard.

Lock the saw out before changing a blade or doing any maintenance.

How to lock it out

  1. Press stop and wait for the blade to stop.
  2. Turn off the saw's power at the yellow service post and put your own lock and tag on it. How the saw is isolated isn't confirmed yet: until a steward marks it, don't do any work that needs it locked out.
  3. Check it's dead: press start. Nothing should happen.
  4. Never open the control box or electrical covers. That's work for a licensed electrician.
  5. To bring it back: tools and rags off the saw, guards back on, take your lock off, then start it with nobody near the blade.

Parts and supplies

  • Blades, scoring blades, parts and service: Taurus Craco in Brampton, the Wood Studio's Martin dealer. 1-800-386-5222, tauruscraco.com.
  • The saw runs at 4,000 revolutions per minute: any blade must be rated for at least that. The fence readouts are set to inches.
  • Ask Taurus Craco for the T70 operating manual: it gives the blade sizes and the maintenance schedule.

Unsafe? Stop it, switch it off, tag it and tell a Wood Studio manager. Then scan the code to report it.

18 inch band saw

Laguna Tools (built in Italy by ACM) LT18 · Machine number 6

Draft for the Laguna band saw's machine stewards to agree at the saw, from Laguna's band saw manual.

Leave it like this

  1. Press the red stop switch and let the wheels stop on their own.
  2. Take the tension off the blade with the tension handwheel. Laguna says to do this whenever the saw won't be used for a while, such as overnight: tension left on makes flat spots in the wheels' rubber tires.
  3. Hang a note on the saw saying the tension is off, so the next person tensions the blade before starting.
  4. Lower the upper blade guard close to the table.
  5. If you changed the blade, put back the table insert and the pin that lines up the two halves of the table.
  6. Brush sawdust off the table. Never clear it by hand near the blade, and never with the air hose.
  7. Put the fence and push sticks back, and close blast gate G9 (the hose from the ceiling).

Before you start

  1. Open blast gate G9 and check the dust collector is running. Laguna asks for at least 1,000 cubic feet per minute.
  2. Close both doors. Check the foot brake is fully up and the red stop switch is pulled out.
  3. Tension the blade if it's slack, then check it's tracking, with the saw unplugged and turning the wheel by hand. Never track the blade with the saw running. Laguna's tension check: at the 9 o'clock point on the top wheel, 6 inches down, moderate thumb pressure should push the blade 3/16 to 1/4 inch sideways.
  4. Check the blade runs freely through the table insert and between the guides.
  5. Set the upper guard just above the wood: about 3 to 6 millimetres (1/8 to 1/4 inch).

Unplug the saw before changing or tracking a blade, or any maintenance. Wear gloves and safety glasses to handle blades.

How to lock it out

  1. Press stop and wait for the wheels to stop.
  2. Pull the plug out of the wall.
  3. Keep the plug where you can see and reach it the whole time you work, or put a plug lock on it with your own lock and tag.
  4. Check it's dead: press start. Nothing should happen.
  5. Never open the switch box or the motor covers. That's work for a licensed electrician.
  6. After a blade breaks, the upper wheel may still be turning: open the doors slowly.
  7. To bring it back: tools off the saw, doors closed, guard set, then plug it in and start it with nobody near the blade.

Parts and supplies

  • Blades: 155 1/2 to 159 inches long, up to 1 1/4 inches wide (from the plate).
  • Lubricant: Teflon-based only. Laguna says ordinary oil attracts dust and dirt.
  • About every 8 hours of use: turn the back ceramic guide about 15 degrees so its groove wears evenly.
  • Regularly (Laguna gives no interval): with the blade off, dress the tires with 100-grit sandpaper while turning the wheels by hand; check the ceramic guides for cracks or chips; check the drive belt for cracks.
  • When the saw isn't in constant use: wax the table and the fence bar. The bearings are sealed for life.
  • Parts: Laguna Tools, Irvine, California. Serial L17030162, built in 2017.

Unsafe? Stop it, switch it off, tag it and tell a Wood Studio manager. Then scan the code to report it.

Drill press

Teknatool Nova Voyager DVR (58000)

Draft for the drill's machine stewards to agree at the drill.

Leave it like this

  1. Stop the drill and let the chuck come to a stop on its own. Don't grab it.
  2. Take the bit out, close the chuck's jaws, and put the chuck key back where it belongs.
  3. Let the handle return all the way up. Undo the knob that locks the handle down, and run the depth stop nut back up to the top.
  4. Put the control panel back to normal:
    • direction set to forward;
    • no drilling depth set;
    • Self-Start off;
    • normal speed: press F2.
    Self-Start makes the motor start on its own when someone pulls the handle down. If it's left on, the next person gets a surprise.
  5. Don't change anything in the menu. Changes there stay for everyone until someone resets the drill to its factory settings.
  6. Leave the table flat (not tilted), locked tight, at the usual height.
  7. Put the drill vise, fence and clamps back.
  8. Vacuum the chips off the drill, the table and the base. Don't use the air hose: dust that gets into the motor's sensors stops the drill.

Before you start

  1. Check the control panel: the speed you want, forward direction, and no drilling depth or Self-Start you didn't set yourself.
  2. Make sure the chuck key isn't in the chuck.
  3. Make sure the table is locked. With the power off, turn the bit by hand once to check it clears the work and the table.
  4. Lower the clear guard over the chuck. If the drill has the guard switch, it won't start with the guard up.
  5. Clamp the work or hold it in the drill vise, never in your hand.

Unplug the drill before doing any maintenance.

How to lock it out

  1. Press OFF and let the chuck stop.
  2. Pull the plug out of the wall.
  3. Keep the plug where you can see and reach it the whole time you work, or put a plug lock on it with your own lock and tag.
  4. Check it's dead: press ON. Nothing should happen.
  5. Unplugging covers waxing, greasing and changing the chuck. Never open the switch box or the rear covers: the maker says parts inside can carry dangerous voltage even when the drill is unplugged. That's work for a licensed electrician.
  6. To bring it back: tools and rags off the drill, guard down, then plug it in and start it with nobody near the chuck.

Parts and supplies

  • Teknatool part numbers:
    • chuck: 8338051;
    • chuck key: 8338052;
    • tapered shaft that holds the chuck in the drill: 8338004;
    • wedge for knocking that shaft out: 8338049.
  • To tilt the table: the wrench that came with the drill (part 8338048) and a 5 millimetre Allen key.
  • Once a month: paste wax on the bare metal surfaces.
  • Twice a year:
    • grease on the gear that raises the table and on the grooves of the spindle shaft;
    • one drop of light oil on the handle's gear.
  • To order parts: Teknatool, or King Canada (the Canadian importer). Check the model number on the drill's plate first: 58000 or 58006.
  • The maker says the drill must be plugged into a surge protector rated at least 15 amps.

Unsafe? Stop it, switch it off, tag it and tell a Wood Studio manager. Then scan the code to report it.

Hinge boring and insertion machine

Blum MiniPress P

Draft for the Blum machine's stewards to agree at the machine, from Blum's MINIPRESS P operating instructions.

Leave it like this

  1. Swing the insertion ram's arm up and release the hold-down clamps.
  2. Clean the work table, the ruler and the stops with a microfibre cloth. Never use compressed air or metal tools, and no oil: the runners and bearings need none.
  3. Set the main switch to 0 at the end of the day, or if it won't be used for a while.
  4. Put the tooling back in the wall cabinet above the machine.

Before you start

  1. Daily, before work and when operators change (Blum): check the air and electrical lines for damage, the glass around the hold-down clamps, and the guards.
  2. Check the air at the machine's own filter-regulator: Blum's range is 5 to 7 bar (73 to 101 psi).
  3. Check the drill bits are sharp, secure and the right way round: counterclockwise bits in the red chucks, clockwise bits in the black ones.
  4. One operator at a time. Keep your hands out of the work area: the hold-down clamps crush.
  5. Drill only wood, particle board or epoxy-coated wood.

Before cleaning or maintenance: main switch to 0, unplug it, and disconnect the air. The main switch doesn't cut the air.

How to lock it out

  1. Set the main switch to 0.
  2. Pull the plug and keep it where you can see and reach it, or put a plug lock on it with your own lock and tag.
  3. Disconnect the air supply: the main switch doesn't disconnect it.
  4. Check it's dead: the operating display stays dark and nothing moves when you press the switches.
  5. Repairs: a Blum partner only, with original Blum parts.
  6. To bring it back: guards on, air connected, plug in, main switch to 1.

Parts and supplies

  • Drill bits: 57 millimetres long, all the same length. Change them with the main switch at 0 and the drilling head unclamped. Don't unscrew a chuck's fixing screw all the way: it can damage the chuck. Fit cover caps on empty chucks.
  • Monthly (Blum): drain water from the air filter-regulator, and wipe the guides, ruler, stops and clamps.
  • Blum notes the machine isn't explosion-proof: keep it away from spraying.
  • Blum reference M53.1053*CA, serial JR 00099, built in 2021. Power: single-phase, 220 volts, 6.5 amps.

Unsafe? Stop it, switch it off, tag it and tell a Wood Studio manager. Then scan the code to report it.

Radial arm saw

DeWalt Inc., Lancaster, Pennsylvania Model to confirm

Draft for the radial arm saw's machine stewards to agree at the saw. There's no manual for it, so this card comes from the program's record and CCOHS radial arm saw guidance.

Leave it like this

  1. Switch off and wait for the blade to stop completely, with the head pushed all the way back behind the fence.
  2. Leave the head at the back of the arm, behind the fence.
  3. Set the arm and blade back to the usual square crosscut position.
  4. Put clamps, stops and push sticks back where they belong.
  5. Brush sawdust off the table and fence. Never clear it by hand near the blade, and never with the air hose.
  6. Close blast gate G8 (to the hood on the bench).

Before you start

  1. Open blast gate G8 and check the dust collector is running.
  2. Check both parts of the guard: the upper hood over the blade and the lower guard that rides on the wood.
  3. Let go of the handle with the head at the back: it mustn't creep forward on its own. If it does, don't use the saw. Report it.
  4. Use only blades and accessories made for this saw. Never use a cracked or dull blade.
  5. Clamp the wood for miter and bevel cuts. No ripping unless the riving knife and anti-kickback devices are fitted and adjusted.
  6. Crosscut from the handle side: pull the head with the hand on the handle, keep your other hand out of line with the blade, and push the head all the way back after every cut.

Unplug the saw before any adjustment or maintenance. Measure only with the saw off.

How to lock it out

  1. Press stop and wait for the blade to stop.
  2. Pull the saw's plug and keep it where you can see and reach it, or put a plug lock on it with your own lock and tag. If the saw turns out to be wired in, lock its disconnect switch off instead.
  3. Check it's dead: press start. Nothing should happen.
  4. Never open the switch box or the motor covers. That's work for a licensed electrician.
  5. To bring it back: tools and rags off the saw, guards back on, then reconnect it and start it with nobody near the blade.

Parts and supplies

  • Parts and manuals for DeWalt-design radial arm saws: The Original Saw Company, which bought DeWalt's rights in 1990. Parts may be hard to find for a saw this age.
  • Motor: single-phase, 3 horsepower, 3,425 revolutions per minute, rated for one hour of continuous running. Any blade must be rated for that speed; give the motor a rest on long jobs.
  • Find the saw's own plate on the column or the base: the model is needed for parts. The motor's serial number ends in 2051098.
