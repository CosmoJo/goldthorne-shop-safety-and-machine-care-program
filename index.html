Status

Colours show each machine's status, like a traffic light. Out of service (red): it can't be used, because it doesn't work or is partly taken apart, the maker says not to use it with its problem, the law requires something before it's used that isn't in place, or a guard, interlock, brake or emergency stop is missing or not working. Use with care (yellow): it can still be used, but has a known issue, named in its record with any maker's instruction about it. Ready to use (green): checked and cleared by the program. Not checked yet (grey): the program hasn't checked it; the Wood Studio's existing rules and sign-offs apply. No status gives permission to use a machine: that depends on training and sign-off. Maintenance status and verification are separate fields in each record. These are proposed conventions awaiting adoption, not legal categories.

          Safety walk

          Once a week, walk the Wood Studio and send the AI Assistant photos of each area: extinguishers, exits, electrical panels, walkways and anything that looks wrong. It spots hazards, like a ladder in front of an extinguisher, and the ones that keep coming back.
